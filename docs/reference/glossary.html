Glossary

Short definitions with direct links that the app and documentation can reuse.

Each heading has a permanent link so the app can point directly to its explanation. Definitions describe concepts rather than where controls sit on the screen.

Air temperature#

The temperature of the air itself; also called dry-bulb temperature.

Mean radiant temperature#

A temperature representing radiant heat exchange with the surrounding surfaces.

Operative temperature#

A combined representation of the air and radiant thermal environment. Its relationship to the two temperatures depends on heat-transfer conditions, including air speed.

Air speed#

The speed of air movement at the occupant.

Relative air speed#

Air speed relative to the body, including movement-related air motion where the model requires it.

Relative humidity#

Water-vapour pressure expressed as a percentage of saturation vapour pressure at the same temperature.

Humidity ratio#

Mass of water vapour per mass of dry air. Conversion to relative humidity also depends on temperature and pressure.

Dew point#

The temperature at which cooling moist air brings it to saturation under the specified pressure conditions.

Wet-bulb temperature#

A temperature associated with evaporative cooling of a wetted sensor in moving air.

Vapour pressure#

The partial pressure contributed by water vapour in the air.

Atmospheric pressure#

The total pressure of the surrounding air, distinct from water-vapour partial pressure.

Metabolic rate#

Activity-related metabolic heat production, commonly expressed in met.

met#

A unit of metabolic rate: 1 met corresponds to 58.15 W/m².

Clothing insulation#

Resistance to heat transfer provided by clothing, commonly expressed in clo.

clo#

A unit of clothing insulation: 1 clo corresponds to 0.155 m²·K/W.

Dynamic clothing insulation#

Clothing insulation adjusted for movement and air effects according to the chosen method.

PMV — Predicted Mean Vote#

A model prediction of average thermal sensation for a group, on the seven-point scale from −3 to +3.

PPD — Predicted Percentage Dissatisfied#

An estimate derived from PMV of the percentage of a group dissatisfied with the thermal environment.

Heat Index#

An apparent-temperature index combining air temperature and relative humidity.

Prevailing mean outdoor temperature#

A summary of recent outdoor temperatures used by the ASHRAE adaptive method.

Acceptability#

Whether a condition lies within the selected model’s acceptance interval. Always identify the method and acceptance level.

Comfort zone#

A region satisfying a specified comfort criterion for stated assumptions. It is not interchangeable with every coloured chart band.

Applicability#

The conditions under which a method is intended to be used. Applicability and whether conditions are comfortable are separate questions.

Out of range#

A value outside a stated range. Identify whether that range is a model applicability limit, an input control range or a chart range; do not infer which from the phrase alone.

Compliance#

Satisfaction of the relevant requirements of a specified standard. A model index alone may not assess all of those requirements.

Model#

A mathematical method for calculating an index or acceptable conditions from inputs.

Standard#

A published specification, such as ASHRAE 55 or ISO 7730. Its edition matters when reporting an assessment.

Edition#

A dated version of a standard. The edition required for an assessment need not be the calculation library’s default.

Band#

A labelled interval used to classify or colour values. State where its thresholds come from.

Axis range#

The extent displayed on a chart. It does not establish a model’s applicability.

Slot#

A set of conditions being considered together. Slot controls vary between application releases.

Entry mode#

A way of supplying a quantity, such as entering humidity as relative humidity or dew point. Available modes depend on the app release.

Input calculator#

A helper that calculates a value for an input. Its application and persistence depend on the release.

Compare#

A presentation of multiple sets of conditions together. Baseline selection and enabled slots are release-specific behaviour.

Session#

The inputs and settings associated with work in the application. Persistence and sharing behaviour depend on the release.

Share link#

An application URL that carries or identifies a shared state. What the link includes depends on the application release.

Additional application vocabulary

Violation#

A condition that breaks a stated applicability or assessment requirement. It should be distinguished from an ordinary result outside a comfort interval.

Bound#

A minimum or maximum used for a check. A one-sided bound has only a minimum or only a maximum.

Classifier#

A rule that assigns an output value to a named category.

Quantity#

A measurable input or output, such as temperature, humidity or metabolic rate.

Activity-adjusted input#

An input adjusted to account for activity, such as relative air speed or dynamic clothing insulation.

Entry group#

Related quantities that can describe the same state in different forms, such as the different humidity quantities.

Preset#

A named reference choice that supplies a value, such as a typical activity or clothing ensemble.

Option#

A model setting that is not a physical quantity, such as whether occupants control air speed.

Page#

An application screen with its own address, such as Standard or Explore.

Session controls#

Settings or actions that apply across the current work rather than to one physical input.

Band list#

A collection of labelled intervals used to describe an output scale.

Edge#

A boundary between adjacent intervals.

Palette#

A set of colours used to distinguish categories or other chart elements.

Scan#

Evaluation of a model across a range or grid of input values.

Swatch#

A small colour sample used to identify a chart or table item.

Image#

A saved graphical representation of a chart.

Input summary#

A description of the values and assumptions used for a calculation.
