Models

Heat Index · Rothfusz

An apparent-temperature index calculated from air temperature and relative humidity.

Basis: the Rothfusz regression, rather than a building thermal-comfort standard.

Source: pythermalcomfort — Model definition and parameter documentation. See also References.

Inputs

InputUnitMeaning
Air temperature°CAmbient air temperature.
Relative humidity%Moisture level of the air.

Output

The primary output is Heat Index, expressed as a temperature. The calculation library also documents a heat-stress category. Report the numeric value and unit; if using a category, identify the source of its thresholds.

Source: pythermalcomfort — return values and applicability. See also References.

Interpretation and limits

The index describes hot conditions under the assumptions of the method. It does not accept clothing, activity or mean radiant temperature as inputs, and it is not a PMV or occupational exposure assessment.

The US National Weather Service explanation describes the operational regression and its adjustments. Do not infer the application’s input gate from a chart axis, or assume that every implementation handles cool conditions in the same way.

A category describes this index’s scale; it does not establish safety for a particular person or exposure.

Source: pythermalcomfort model documentation.
