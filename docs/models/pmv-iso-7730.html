Models

PMV / PPD (ISO 7730, Category B)

pmv-isoISO 7730 workspaceExplore

The same Fanger heat-balance model described on the ASHRAE 55 page, evaluated under ISO 7730. The equations agree; what differs is the applicability range, how the result is banded, and which provisions the standard allows.

Six primary factors affect thermal comfort. Four describe the environment air temperature, mean radiant temperature, air speed and humidity and two describe the person: metabolic rate and clothing insulation.

PMV uses all six, and the descriptions of each factor on the ASHRAE page apply here unchanged. This page covers what is specific to the ISO treatment.

How it differs from the ASHRAE variant

ISO 7730 ASHRAE 55
Occupant control of air speed No such setting Available, and changes the applicability limits
Clothing applicability Up to 2 clo Up to 1.5 clo
Chart banding on its workspace The seven sensation categories Acceptable / not acceptable
Comfort boundary drawn The edges of the Neutral category The standard's acceptable PMV limits
Operative temperature ISO formulation ASHRAE formulation
The elevated-air-speed cooling effect belongs to the ASHRAE model. Do not read an ISO result as though a fan had been accounted for.

Environmental factors

Air temperature and mean radiant temperature are accepted from 10 to 40 °C, relative air speed from 0 to 2 m/s, and relative humidity across the full 0 to 100 % range. Humidity can be entered in any of the five modes relative humidity, humidity ratio, dew point, wet bulb or vapour pressure and temperature in separate or operative mode.

Air speed is the relative air speed, so the measured-air-speed modifier applies the same correction for the occupant's own movement as it does on the ASHRAE model.

Personal factors

Metabolic rate is accepted from 1 to 4 met. Clothing insulation extends to 2 clo, wider than ASHRAE 55 allows the main practical difference between the two, since a heavily dressed occupant may be assessable here and out of range there.

All four input modifiers are available: measured air speed, morning clothing estimate, dynamic clothing and solar gain.

Outputs

Output Meaning
PMV Predicted mean vote, −3 to +3.
PPD Predicted percentage dissatisfied.
Sensation category Cold, Cool, Slightly Cool, Neutral, Slightly Warm, Warm or Hot.

Banding and the comfort boundary

ISO 7730 does not define a single acceptable/unacceptable band the way ASHRAE 55 does, so the workspace bands the chart by the seven sensation categories and treats Neutral as the comfort criterion. The boundary lines drawn on the chart are the edges of that category.

Categories of thermal environment

ISO 7730 describes categories of thermal environment commonly A, B and C with progressively tighter PMV limits. The tool evaluates at the category B expectation, which is the ordinary design case and the reason this model is named as it is. For a different category, compare the reported PMV against that category's limit directly.

Edition

The model is evaluated against the 7730:2005 edition. Where the calculation library offers a newer default, this edition is pinned deliberately so results stay comparable with the published literature and with earlier versions of this tool.

Applicability

Inputs outside the standard's stated range are marked and reported as out of range rather than given a verdict.

Where to find it

On the ISO 7730 workspace, which offers this model only, and in Explore.
