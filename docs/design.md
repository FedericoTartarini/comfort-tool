# Design

The look of the tool: what [ADR-0002](adr/0002-library-interface-model-info.md) decision 73 and its predecessors (65
to 72) record as rules, drawn out. Approved with decision 73 on 2026-10-09, after the `prototype/shadcn-shell`
prototype (`.scratch/prototype-shadcn-shell/notes.md`, rounds 1 to 29; the branch at `f82fdc2` is the reference, kept
and never merged). It binds the tickets as the decisions do; on a conflict the ADR wins. The Phase 5c spec's §Design
plan (`.scratch/interface-design/spec.md`) is the previous look, kept as history.

## Subject and job

A calculator for thermal comfort used by HVAC engineers, researchers and students, made by the Center for the Built
Environment. Change one input, read the verdict and the chart. The chart is the hero; everything around it is quiet.
The user's ask, in translation: an elegant sense of design that still fits a rigorous research tool, one coherent
design language, and a style without the look of generated AI output. The reading agreed with the prototype: an instrument panel, not a product dashboard. References by
name: Dieter Rams (unobtrusive, thorough down to the last detail, as little design as possible); Apple's grouped
panels (System Settings, Finder: white groups on a barely tinted ground, no shadow, one radius, equal padding);
Tufte's smallest effective difference. Not references: SaaS dashboards, stat tiles, badges, trend arrows, decorative
icons, cream grounds with warm accents, display fonts.

## Principles

1. **Colour means data.** Hue appears where it carries information: a slot's zones and marker, a band's fill, a
   category's swatch. The interface is grey, white and black. The one blue, the brand, is spent on the current
   navigation item, the primary button, the focus ring, links, the header's and footer's bands, and every chosen or
   pressed toggle; red marks a box whose draft is past its bound or would be refused, once the typing pauses, and a
   refused Edge's reason; a violation is marked by a button with an icon on its result row and by words in ink, never
   a hue (decision 84).
2. **Structure is information.** The modules are white panels on a faint ground, each bounded by a ring at 10 % ink;
   no shadows, no gradients; one radius. A rule is drawn only inside a panel (table rows, the Inputs card's under the
   model), inside a dialog (the Input calculators' result dock) and in the sidebar (above Settings, and above
   Share). The header and the footer are bands of the brand. A boundary encodes a boundary that exists.
3. **Instrument density.** Body text at 14 px; numbers in tabular figures so decimals align; labels in sentence case,
   never upper case. Line length under 80 characters in any prose the page carries.
4. **No motion that nobody asked for.** Dialogs and sheets keep the primitive's; nothing else animates.
5. **Layout stability** (decision 67, rule 10). At one viewport no control changes a region's width; a control changes
   content only inside its own region and below itself. Compare reflows inside a constant `28.75rem` input card, and
   the root reserves the scrollbar's gutter, so the scrollbar Compare adds moves nothing sideways (decision 84, rule
   12); no control moves the plot sideways or resizes it, and Compare's result rows, arriving above it, may move it
   down; the legend, whose entries Compare adds, re-centres in its room.
6. **Official parts, then small changes** (decision 73, rule 1). Every part with a counterpart in shadcn-svelte's
   registry is generated by the CLI, a primitive or a block, and adjusted at the use site or by `data-slot`; a
   primitive is never hand-edited; a block's files may be, and record what changed. Hand-made only where the registry
   has nothing: the footer, the legend and the Swatch, the Plotly host, the layout helpers, the two marks; and the
   result table's scroll region, by decision 70.
7. **The fewest values, each with one role** (decision 73, rule 2). The user's rule, in translation: the whole interface uses one gap,
   never a mix of several gaps or of several type sizes and faces, unless one is necessary. Every value below has one role; an exception is named by its necessity.

66's rules. The sidebar primitive's eight tokens derive from these. The slot hues and the band colours are data and
keep their colours whatever the chrome does.

## Type

Geist Variable, one family. Three sizes with one role each: the title at 18 px / 600; body, controls, labels and the
module headings at 14 px (400; 500 for a label and the current navigation item; 600 for a module heading); a caption,
a description and the range text at 12 px in `inkMuted`. Plus the generated components' own sizes: Nova's `sm` button
and toggle letter at 12.8 px, kept because the default size costs about 4 px a labelled row and with it the one-screen
fit. The result table's numbers in `tabular-nums`, right-aligned. Line height 1.5. Plotly's lettering is in px, by
its API: tick labels 12 px, the hover readout at the caption's 12 px in Geist.

## Spacing and radius

Two spacing values for the page: `0.75rem` for the page gutter, the gap between modules, a panel's padding, between
rows and within a row (the layout helpers' `gap="4"` and `gap="2"` read this token); `0.25rem` within a field, between
its label, its box and its caption (`gap="1"`). The header is `3.5rem` tall. A generated component keeps its style's
own insides (a sidebar group's `p-2`, a menu's `gap-1`, a button's padding): those are the registry's, not the page's.
One radius token, 6 px, on every bordered control whatever its size, the `sm` and `xs` buttons, the toggles and the
input-group's buttons among them (decision 73, rule 2 as revised 2026-10-12); Nova's derived steps
(`--radius-sm/md/xl`) are kept only for the items inside a padded popup, which need a smaller corner than the popup. Units are rem, except hairlines (1 px borders, the 2 px line swatch), the
radius steps, values read off the DOM and Plotly's px API.

root reserves (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`). A fixed sidebar of `14rem` on the ground; beside it the main area, `0.75rem`
gutter, with the input card at `28.75rem` and the result and chart cards taking the rest, `0.75rem` between them; the
header and the footer bands full width above and below, neither resident. The sidebar's fills, the header's mark and the
footer's text share one 12 px line; the sidebar's first group label's top meets the cards' top.

**Header.** A band of the brand: the white CBE mark, a link to the default model's address, followed as an in-app
switch that keeps the values and asks where one is out of range (decision 73, rule 5 as revised again 2026-10-12); a
vertical separator; the title, an `h1`; the room between, empty, the header holding no notice (decision 84, rule 7);
Documentation. Below `md` the sidebar's trigger stands before the mark. Focus after Reset or a
link's yes lands on the `h1`.

**Sidebar.** The `sidebar-16` block's menu on the ground, no icons, items `gap-1`. Groups: Standard, one item per
standard; Tools, Explore (Time-series joins as one item when it exists, no placeholder); a separator; Settings, the
unit system as a toggle-group, the atmospheric pressure's input with its bound text, a separator, Share and Reset,
standing on the group labels' line, 20 px in, text against text; the pressure's box is the paper, since a box takes
the other colour of the surface it stands on. The current address is drawn as current; a standard's link on another of
its models too (decision 67, rule 2).

**Inputs.** A card: Compare, a switch with its label, at the heading row's right end on Standard (decision 76); the
model select, a rule, the quantity rows, and the Input calculators button. A row is a label with its unit, the entry
group's menu button beside it where the row belongs to a group (decision 72), the box with its Presets menu button
inside it where the row has presets (decision 67, rule 3), and the line under it, the bound with its unit
(`10 – 40 °C`), which changes neither colour nor words. The box is a text input that takes only a number; at its right end stand
its two step buttons, chevron up over chevron down, and after them, flush with its edge, the Presets button (decision
84, rule 10 and its note of 2026-10-27). The Presets button reads "Presets" at every Compare state, `0.5rem` of room
each side of the word; its list's values end at one x, and a preset past the bound, or one that would put another value
out of range, is dimmed and disabled, its number shown. A draft past its bound, or one that would be refused, turns the
box's edge red once the typing pauses, never while it is typed. An option is a switch with its label, tucked under the
row it qualifies at the field gap, PMV (ASHRAE 55)'s under the air speed (decision 36); with Compare on, one switch per
slot column, "On" or "Off" beside it. The box is tinted `ground`; a select's trigger is not. With Compare on, the three
slot buttons head three columns of the same `28.75rem` (decision 50); every pressed toggle is filled with the brand. The
card ends with the Input calculators button, full width, outline, "Input calculators" over the names of the calculators
the model declares, which opens one tabbed dialog of one fixed height (decision 75); a model that declares none has no
button.

**Results.** A card: the Results heading; the table (decision 70: headings wrap, numbers right-aligned, the first
column sticky, the region scrolls sideways with its clipped edge faded); then the standard's caption. A row whose run
broke a limit carries a ghost button with the triangle-alert icon, named "Limits broken": before the slot's swatch in
the Input column while the slots are named, else first in its Compliance cell, else in its first value cell. Activated,
not hovered, it opens a popover, "Calculated, but outside these limits:" over the limits in their bound sentences
(decision 84, rule 8). A "—" a violation causes has no icon of its own, and an output's broken bound is a caption in
its Compliance cell too.

**Chart.** A card: the heading row carries Chart, then at the right, on Explore, the Bands button, and Export image;
under it a view row, on every chart, holds the chart type toggle-group, named "Chart type", then the axis selects where
the chart has them, left-aligned (decision 73, rule 8 as revised 2026-10-12). The plot is the exported image's 4:3 plot
area, capped at `24rem` tall, so the screen and the file are one picture; it stands on the card's leading edge, and no
control moves it; content above the chart card may (Compare's rows). On Standard, a slot whose Comfort zones are all
empty has a caption under the plot and the legend, "No comfort zone at these inputs.", named with its swatch under
Compare; the legend keeps the zones' entries (decision 83, rule 1). The legend is a column beside it, `10rem` wide by
preference, centred in a room of at most `18rem` to its right, entries wrapping to lines. Bands is a dialog, `40rem` as
every dialog (decision 67, rule 9), opened from the heading row. It edits a draft of the Band list, which the chart does
not draw: its footer holds Reset bands at the left and Apply, the primary button, at the right, and the corner's X and
Escape discard the draft. An Edge typed out of order is refused when it is committed, its reason in red under its box
until that box is typed in again; the box is not marked, as it shows the Edge as it was (decision 84, rule 6). The
readout stays on the pointer, lettered at the caption's size.

**Toasts.** Every notice is a toast, `svelte-sonner`'s, at the window's top right at Sonner's own offset (24 px, 16
px under 600 px), over the header's right end while the page is at its top; on the popover's ground with the border's
edge, kept until its close button, "Close", closes it, Sonner showing three at once (decision 84, rule 7, and its notes
of 2026-10-28). The notices: a link refused or filled, values cut by an arrival, a copy refused, an Image that failed,
a value refused with its reason, and a slot's run breaking a limit it did not break before. A typed value's cut and an
input calculator's Apply raise none. Reset closes the toasts standing; a link's yes closes none.

**Footer.** A band of the brand: the tool's name and version, the library and its version, Licence and Code on one
line, the citation on the next, at the left; the white CBE and UC Berkeley marks at the right, links to
cbe.berkeley.edu and berkeley.edu. No date.

**One screen.** At the values above and 1440 × 780, every model's result and chart cards are in view, and the input
card is not on the two PMV pages: measured 2026-10-28, PMV (ASHRAE 55)'s input card ends at 856 px and PMV (ISO
7730)'s at 817, Adaptive's at 543, while ISO PMV's chart card, the lowest, ends at 770 (it was ASHRAE PMV's input
column at 767 before decision 84; the change is not bisected). A taller plot only lengthens the page, so the cap is
the largest plot that keeps the chart cards in view at that floor.

## Narrower windows

The layout is intrinsic (Every Layout's Sidebar; MDN's "change the design at the point where the content starts to look
bad"): the app writes no `@media` breakpoint of its own, and nothing reads the legend's content, so at one viewport no
control moves anything. In the chart card the plot comes first, its `inline-size` its full width (four thirds of the
cap) with `flex: none` and `max-inline-size: 100%`, so it shrinks only when the window is narrower than it; the legend
is a column with `flex-basis: 10rem`, growing into a room of at most `18rem`, its entries wrapping; when the room is
narrower than that basis the legend wraps under the plot. Two columns, the input card beside the result and chart cards,
while the main area is at least `74.5rem` wide, a container query in rem derived from the content (the input card, the
module gap, the chart card's padding, the plot at its cap, its gap to the legend, the legend's basis and the gutter),
measured against the main area, so 1416 px of window with the sidebar, 1431 px where the scrollbar's gutter takes 15
px; narrower, the result and chart cards stand under the input card at the main area's width, and the input card keeps
its `28.75rem` at the left (decision 84, rule 12), the legend beside the plot down to 944 px. Below `md`, at 767 px and
narrower by the primitive's own check, the sidebar is the primitive's `Sheet`, the one device breakpoint, its trigger in
the header before the mark, with the Settings group inside it; at 768 px it is still fixed. Compare's three slot columns
stay side by side at every width (decision 50). Wider than the content needs, the page stops growing: the sidebar and
the main area are one frame, the sidebar's `14rem` and the main area's `76rem`, its width at 1440 px, centred in the
window, so the chart card, the table's columns and the legend's room keep the 1440 px proportions on a wide monitor; the
header's and footer's contents stand in the same frame, their bands' ground full width, as the deployed tool's are
(decision 73, rule 10 as revised again 2026-10-12). Verified by a screenshot walk at 2560, 1920, 1440, 1366, 1280, 1024,
768 and 767 px, and held by the layout's browser test (`tests/browser/layout.behaviour.ts`); since decision 84, 1366 px
is one column. A window narrower than the input card overflows sideways. At 768 px Dynamic's view
row wraps to two lines, so a chart type switch there moves the plot down. A phone, 375 px, scrolls sideways; it is a
later pass.

## Self-check against the generic

Removed from the earlier look: the bare white-on-white frame, the column rules, the navigation's icons, the empty line
reserved for Time-series. Not used: shadows, stat tiles, badges, eyebrow labels, a collapsible sidebar, a cream ground
with a warm accent, a monospace face for data. Kept and recorded: the middle dot the copy uses as a separator; the
generated components' own insides. What is this tool's: principle 1, hue only on data, with one blue for the chrome; and
a chart shape shared by the screen and the exported file.
