# Design

The look of the tool: what [ADR-0002](adr/0002-library-interface-model-info.md) decision 73 and its predecessors (65
to 72) record as rules, drawn out. Approved with decision 73 on 2026-10-09, after the `prototype/shadcn-shell`
prototype (`.scratch/prototype-shadcn-shell/notes.md`, rounds 1 to 29; the branch at `f82fdc2` is the reference, kept
and never merged). It binds the tickets as the decisions do; on a conflict the ADR wins. The Phase 5c spec's §Design
plan (`.scratch/interface-design/spec.md`) is the previous look, kept as history.

## Subject and job

A calculator for thermal comfort used by HVAC engineers, researchers and students, made by the Center for the Built
Environment. Change one input, read the verdict and the chart. The chart is the hero; everything around it is quiet.
The user's ask, in translation: an elegant sense of design that still fits a rigorous research tool, one coherent
design language, and a style without the look of generated AI output. The reading agreed with the prototype: an instrument panel, not a product dashboard. References by
name: Dieter Rams (unobtrusive, thorough down to the last detail, as little design as possible); Apple's grouped
panels (System Settings, Finder: white groups on a barely tinted ground, no shadow, one radius, equal padding);
Tufte's smallest effective difference. Not references: SaaS dashboards, stat tiles, badges, trend arrows, decorative
icons, cream grounds with warm accents, display fonts.

## Principles

1. **Colour means data.** Hue appears where it carries information: a slot's zones and marker, a band's fill, a
   category's swatch. The interface is grey, white and black. The one blue, the brand, is spent on the current
   navigation item, the primary button, the focus ring, links, the header's and footer's bands, and every chosen or
   pressed toggle; red marks an entry out of range, a refused Edge and a kept row's note; a kept result is greyed,
   never coloured.
2. **Structure is information.** The modules are white panels on a faint ground, each bounded by a ring at 10 % ink;
   no shadows, no gradients; one radius. A rule is drawn only inside a panel (table rows) and in the sidebar (above
   Session). The header and the footer are bands of the brand. A boundary encodes a boundary that exists.
3. **Instrument density.** Body text at 14 px; numbers in tabular figures so decimals align; labels in sentence case,
   never upper case. Line length under 80 characters in any prose the page carries.
4. **No motion that nobody asked for.** Dialogs and sheets keep the primitive's; nothing else animates.
5. **Layout stability** (decision 67, rule 10). At one viewport no control changes a region's width; a control changes
   content only inside its own region and below itself. Compare reflows inside a constant `24rem` input column; the
   plot never moves; the legend, whose entries Compare adds, re-centres in its room.
6. **Official parts, then small changes** (decision 73, rule 1). Every part with a counterpart in shadcn-svelte's
   registry is generated by the CLI, a primitive or a block, and adjusted at the use site or by `data-slot`; a
   primitive is never hand-edited; a block's files may be, and record what changed. Hand-made only where the registry
   has nothing: the footer, the legend and the Swatch, the Plotly host, the layout helpers, the two marks; and the
   result table's scroll region, by decision 70.
7. **The fewest values, each with one role** (decision 73, rule 2). The user's rule, in translation: the whole interface uses one gap,
   never a mix of several gaps or of several type sizes and faces, unless one is necessary. Every value below has one role; an exception is named by its necessity.

keep their colours whatever the chrome does.

## Type

Geist Variable, one family. Three sizes with one role each: the title at 18 px / 600; body, controls, labels and the
module headings at 14 px (400; 500 for a label and the current navigation item; 600 for a module heading); a caption,
a description and the range text at 12 px in `inkMuted`. Plus the generated components' own sizes: Nova's `sm` button
and toggle letter at 12.8 px, kept because the default size costs about 4 px a labelled row and with it the one-screen
fit. The result table's numbers in `tabular-nums`, right-aligned. Line height 1.5. Plotly's lettering is in px, by
its API: tick labels 12 px, the hover readout at the caption's 12 px in Geist.

## Spacing and radius

Two spacing values for the page: `0.75rem` for the page gutter, the gap between modules, a panel's padding, between
rows and within a row (the layout helpers' `gap="4"` and `gap="2"` read this token); `0.25rem` within a field, between
its label, its box and its caption (`gap="1"`). The header is `3.5rem` tall. A generated component keeps its style's
own insides (a sidebar group's `p-2`, a menu's `gap-1`, a button's padding): those are the registry's, not the page's.
One radius token, 6 px; Nova's derived steps (`--radius-sm/md/xl`) are kept, since an item inside a padded popup
needs a smaller corner than the popup. Units are rem, except hairlines (1 px borders, the 2 px line swatch), the
radius steps, values read off the DOM and Plotly's px API.

full width above and below, neither resident. The sidebar's fills, the header's mark and the footer's text share one

**Header.** A band of the brand: the white CBE mark, a link to cbe.berkeley.edu; a vertical separator; the title, an
`h1`; the notice line, the generated `Alert` inside the status region, centred in the room between; Documentation.
Focus after Reset or a link's yes lands on the `h1`.

**Sidebar.** The `sidebar-16` block's menu on the ground, no icons, items `gap-1`. Groups: Standard, one item per
standard; Tools, Explore (Time-series joins as one item when it exists, no placeholder); a separator; Session, the
unit system as a toggle-group, the atmospheric pressure's input with its bound text, Copy link and Reset. The current
address is drawn as current; a standard's link on another of its models too (decision 67, rule 2).

**Inputs.** A card: the model select, Compare, the quantity rows. A row is a label with its unit, the entry group's
menu button beside it where the row belongs to a group (decision 72), the box with its Presets menu button inside it
where the row has presets (decision 67, rule 3), and the caption line, `preset · bound`. The box is tinted `ground`;
a select's trigger is not. With Compare on, the three slot buttons head three columns of the same `24rem` (decision
50); every pressed toggle is filled with the brand.

**Results.** A card: the Results heading, the table (decision 70: headings wrap, numbers right-aligned, the first
column sticky, the region scrolls sideways with its clipped edge faded), then the standard's caption and the kept
rows' note, an `Alert` in the `destructive` variant with a triangle-alert icon; a kept row's numbers are muted.

**Chart.** A card: the heading row carries Chart, then at the right the chart type toggle-group, on Explore the
Bands button, and Export image; where the chart has axis selects they take a row of their own, left-aligned. The
plot is the exported image's 4:3 plot area, capped at `24rem` tall, so the screen and the file are one picture; it
stands on the card's leading edge and never moves. The legend is a column beside it, `10rem` wide by preference, centred
in a room of at most `18rem` to its right, entries wrapping to lines. Bands is a dialog, `40rem` as every dialog (decision 67, rule 9),
opened from the heading row; what the dialog does is decided apart (`.scratch/open-points.md` B6). The readout stays
on the pointer, lettered at the caption's size.

**Footer.** A band of the brand: the tool's name and version, the library and its version, Licence and Code on one
line, the citation on the next, at the left; the white CBE and UC Berkeley marks at the right, links to
cbe.berkeley.edu and berkeley.edu. No date.

**One screen.** At the values above, every model's inputs, result and chart are in view at 1440 × 780 (ASHRAE PMV's
input column binds at 767 px of window height); a taller plot only lengthens the page, so the cap is the largest plot
at that floor.

## Narrower windows

The layout is intrinsic (Every Layout's Sidebar; MDN's "change the design at the point where the content starts to
look bad"): the app writes no `@media` breakpoint of its own, and nothing reads the legend's content, so at one
viewport no control moves anything. In the chart card the plot comes first, its `min-inline-size` its full width
(four thirds of the cap) and `max-inline-size: 100%`, so it shrinks only when the window is narrower than it; the
legend is a column with `flex-basis: 10rem`, growing into a room of at most `18rem`, its entries wrapping; when the
room is narrower than that basis the legend wraps under the plot. Two columns, the input card beside the result and
chart cards, while the main area is at least `70rem` wide, a container query in rem derived from the content (the
input column, the module gap, the chart card's padding, the plot at its cap, its gap to the legend, the legend's
basis and the gutter), about 1356 px of window; narrower, the input card stands above the other two and the chart
card takes the main area's width, the legend beside the plot down to about 932 px. Below `md` (768 px) the sidebar
is the primitive's `Sheet`, the one device breakpoint, its trigger in the header before the mark, with the Session
group inside it. Compare's three slot columns stay side by side at every width (decision 50). Verified by a
screenshot walk at 1440, 1366, 1280, 1024 and 768 px.

## Self-check against the generic

Removed from the earlier look: the bare white-on-white frame, the column rules, the navigation's icons, the empty
line reserved for Time-series. Not used: shadows, stat tiles, badges, eyebrow labels, a collapsible sidebar, a toast
for the notice, a cream ground with a warm accent, a monospace face for data. Kept and recorded: the middle dot the
copy uses as a separator; the generated components' own insides. What is this tool's: principle 1, hue only on data,
with one blue for the chrome; and a chart shape shared by the screen and the exported file.
